How to send photos

  1. Name each file after its code, e.g. GRD-01.jpg. For before/after sliders send two files: BLD-C2-before.jpg and BLD-C2-after.jpg.
  2. Send the largest original you have (JPG or PNG). I resize and compress them for the web.
  3. Real Aerata photos only: no AI images or stock photos of other companies. For client sites, check that the client is fine with the photo being public.
  4. Start with the sector cards and heroes: they appear on several pages at once.
